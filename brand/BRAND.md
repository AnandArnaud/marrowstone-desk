# Marrowstone Desk brand

Marrowstone Desk is a help desk for small B2B teams. The brand is calm, direct and dependable.

## Colours

| Role | Hex |
| --- | --- |
| Tide (primary, buttons, links) | `#1F5F7A` |
| Slate (headings, body text) | `#1E2A32` |
| Fog (page background) | `#F4F6F7` |
| Shell (cards, surfaces) | `#FFFFFF` |
| Pebble (borders, dividers) | `#D9DFE3` |
| Kelp (success, resolved states) | `#2E7D5B` |
| Ember (urgent, overdue) | `#C2503D` |

Light backgrounds, dark text. Tide is the only saturated colour in a composition; Kelp and Ember
mark state, never decoration.

## Type

Headings: a humanist sans, tight letter spacing (the app uses the system sans). Body: the same
family at a comfortable size. Numbers in tables are tabular.

## Logo

`logo.svg` is a rounded Tide square with a white horizon line. Minimum size 24 px. Keep clear space
of half the mark's width around it. Do not recolour it.

## Voice

Plain, specific, calm. Say what happened and what to do next. No exclamation marks, no superlatives.

## Imagery

Wide (16:9) images for article headers: a single abstract shape or an object on a Fog background,
Tide as the one accent, generous margins, no people, no text in the image.
