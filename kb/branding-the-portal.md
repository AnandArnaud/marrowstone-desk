This article explains branding the portal. It applies to every plan unless stated otherwise.

## Steps

1. Open **Settings** from the inbox header.
2. Find the section named in the title of this article.
3. Make the change and press **Save**. Changes apply immediately.
4. Open a ticket to confirm the change took effect.

## Good to know

- This setting is per organization, not per queue.
- If a step is missing on your screen, your role may not allow it; ask an admin.

## Questions

**Can agents do this, or only admins?**

Admins always can. Agents can when the section is listed under their role.

**Is this available on the Starter plan?**

Yes, unless the article says otherwise.
