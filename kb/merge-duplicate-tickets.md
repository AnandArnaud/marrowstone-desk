This article explains merge duplicate tickets. It applies to every plan unless stated otherwise.

## Steps

1. Open **Settings** from the inbox header.
2. Choose the organization you are working in.
3. Find the section named in the title of this article.
4. Make the change and press **Save**. Changes apply immediately.

## Good to know

- Customers in the portal are not notified of settings changes.
- If a step is missing on your screen, your role may not allow it; ask an admin.

## Questions

**Can agents do this, or only admins?**

Admins always can. Agents can when the section is listed under their role.

**Does this affect existing tickets?**

No. Existing tickets keep their current values; new tickets use the new setting.
