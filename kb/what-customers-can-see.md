Here is how what customers can see works in Marrowstone Desk, step by step.

## Steps

1. Open **Settings** from the inbox header.
2. Choose the organization you are working in.
3. Find the section named in the title of this article.
4. Make the change and press **Save**. Changes apply immediately.
5. Open a ticket to confirm the change took effect.

## Good to know

- Admins can undo any change from the same screen.
- This setting is per organization, not per queue.

## Questions

**Is this available on the Starter plan?**

Yes, unless the article says otherwise.

**Can agents do this, or only admins?**

Admins always can. Agents can when the section is listed under their role.
