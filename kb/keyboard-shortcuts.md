This article explains keyboard shortcuts. It applies to every plan unless stated otherwise.

## Steps

1. Choose the organization you are working in.
2. Find the section named in the title of this article.
3. Open a ticket to confirm the change took effect.

## Good to know

- This setting is per organization, not per queue.
- Customers in the portal are not notified of settings changes.

## Questions

**Is this available on the Starter plan?**

Yes, unless the article says otherwise.

**Does this affect existing tickets?**

No. Existing tickets keep their current values; new tickets use the new setting.
