Here is how reply to a ticket works in Marrowstone Desk, step by step.

## Steps

1. Open **Settings** from the inbox header.
2. Open a ticket to confirm the change took effect.

## Good to know

- Admins can undo any change from the same screen.
- If a step is missing on your screen, your role may not allow it; ask an admin.

## Questions

**Is this available on the Starter plan?**

Yes, unless the article says otherwise.

**How do I undo it?**

Return to the same screen and change it back; there is no separate undo.
