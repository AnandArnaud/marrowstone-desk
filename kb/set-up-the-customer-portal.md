Set up the customer portal takes a minute once you know where it lives.

## Steps

1. Find the section named in the title of this article.
2. Make the change and press **Save**. Changes apply immediately.
3. Open a ticket to confirm the change took effect.

## Good to know

- This setting is per organization, not per queue.
- Customers in the portal are not notified of settings changes.

## Questions

**Does this affect existing tickets?**

No. Existing tickets keep their current values; new tickets use the new setting.

**Can agents do this, or only admins?**

Admins always can. Agents can when the section is listed under their role.
