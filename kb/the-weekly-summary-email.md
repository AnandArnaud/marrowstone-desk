Here is how the weekly summary email works in Marrowstone Desk, step by step.

## Steps

1. Choose the organization you are working in.
2. Find the section named in the title of this article.
3. Open a ticket to confirm the change took effect.

## Good to know

- Admins can undo any change from the same screen.
- Customers in the portal are not notified of settings changes.

## Questions

**How do I undo it?**

Return to the same screen and change it back; there is no separate undo.

**Can agents do this, or only admins?**

Admins always can. Agents can when the section is listed under their role.
