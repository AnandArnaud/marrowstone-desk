This article explains connect an email address. It applies to every plan unless stated otherwise.

## Steps

1. Choose the organization you are working in.
2. Find the section named in the title of this article.
3. Make the change and press **Save**. Changes apply immediately.
4. Open a ticket to confirm the change took effect.

## Good to know

- Admins can undo any change from the same screen.
- Agents see this change the next time they load the inbox.

## Questions

**How do I undo it?**

Return to the same screen and change it back; there is no separate undo.

**Does this affect existing tickets?**

No. Existing tickets keep their current values; new tickets use the new setting.
